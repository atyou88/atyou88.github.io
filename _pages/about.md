<span id="about"></span>
I am a master's student in Computer Technology at [Nanjing University](https://www.nju.edu.cn/), where I am a member of the [Websoft Research Group](https://ws.nju.edu.cn/), advised by [Prof. Gong Cheng](https://ws.nju.edu.cn/~gcheng). I received my bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Technology from [Xiamen University](https://www.xmu.edu.cn/) in 2025. In the same year, I was admitted to study for a master's degree at Nanjing University without an entrance examination.

<span id="research"></span>
My research interests include **Information Retrieval**, **LLM Agents**, **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)**, and **Knowledge Graphs**.

I aspire to understand technology deeply and use it to change the world for the better.
